# Direction: "You are the training data"

AI Slop Arena is a survivor-like where the enemy is a model being trained on you. The joke
and the depth are the same thing: AI concepts are mechanics, not just names.

## The test for every new feature

1. **Does it make someone laugh, or change a decision?** If neither, it does not go in.
2. **Can a new player ignore it and still have fun?** Depth is there to be found.
3. **Does it stay out of the way?** Nothing covers the screen or takes control; effects
   are shown, not announced.

## Easy to pick up

Move, dash, pick one of three. Temperature 0.2 is a calm first run. Everything else can be
learned later, or never.

## Hard to master

- **Overfitting.** The HUD says what the model resists and what it is weak to, and
  level-up cards say what that means for each weapon. A one-weapon build teaches it to
  resist you; a player who reads it punishes it.
- **Temperature.** The difficulty, as the model's own parameter: hotter means more slop,
  more loot, and hallucinations (fake Likes that blow up).
- **Merges, rarities and glitches** make builds; the model's learning makes you change
  them.

## Funny, because it is about you

- **The model card** at the end of a run is written from what really happened: your
  biases, your limitations, what deleted you.
- **The Nemesis:** what deleted you last comes back by name, and it starts the next run
  ahead on your favourite damage type. It remembers between runs.
- **Prompt Injection:** "Ignore previous instructions" turns the slop on each other.

## Where it goes next

In order, each step only if the playtest says the last one works:

1. **Tune.** A first run at 0.2 should reach the second boss; the default should be a
   fair fight to about 20 minutes.
2. **More AI as mechanics:** The Model refusing to die ("I can't help with that"), rate
   limits that stall enemies, a context window that makes bosses forget their phase,
   hallucinated enemies at high Temperature, wrong tooltips from an unreliable narrator.
3. **Permanent progress:** banked Clout for upgrades between runs, heroes and weapons
   unlocked by jokes as much as by feats, a bestiary of everything deleted.
4. **Real art:** generated sprite sheets loaded in place of the drawn sprites.

No more piles of items. Fewer, stranger things.
